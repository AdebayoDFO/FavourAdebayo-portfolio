# About Me

I'm a technical writer with a background in healthcare, currently studying medicine at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto. My approach is straightforward: take complex technical information and turn it into content that's easy to understand and act on.

## Technical Writing

Technical writing is the core of what I do. I focus on making information findable and usable — whether that's a step-by-step guide, a tool's documentation, or a full docs site.

I have a growing focus on **docs-as-code**: writing documentation in Markdown, managing it with Git, and using GitHub Actions to automate the build and deployment of documentation sites with Docusaurus. It's a workflow that treats documentation the way engineers treat software — versioned, reviewed, and shipped.

<details>
<summary><strong>My approach, in short</strong></summary>

- Start from what the reader is trying to *do*, not just what the feature *is*
- Structure content so people can scan it, not just read it top to bottom
- Keep the same rigor whether it's a quick guide or a full reference doc

</details>

## Graphic Design

Alongside writing, I work in graphic design — developing visual identities and design concepts that communicate ideas clearly at a glance. I'm drawn to the same problem in both disciplines: organizing visuals and information so people can find what matters quickly, whether that's in a workflow doc or a poster.

## Medical Background

I'm currently studying medicine at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto. That training shapes how I write — precision matters, and so does making dense information accessible without dumbing it down.
